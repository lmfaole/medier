# AGENTS.md — film

> **Serving (changed 2026-11):** this app is no longer a Cloudflare Worker with a published snapshot. It is Astro on Node, one of the eight media apps in the single server on the owner's machine (`scripts/media-server.ts`, the `media` container, `docs/MEDIA-SERVER.md`): dynamic server rendering, the database opened read-only, behind the owner's Nextcloud login (so "public" below means "behind that login"). Built with `npm run media:build`, live after `systemctl --user restart media`. There is no `<app>-publish` timer, no `npm run deploy` and no `wrangler.jsonc` any more; where this file says snapshot, publish or Worker for this app, read it as the server's live read of `.data/`. The sync timers stay.

`sky.lmfaole.party/apps/medier/film`: the films the owner has watched and what the film register TMDB says about them, a snapshot of
`.data/film.db`. Public and not indexed (the three layers, as music). Rules shared with every app are in the root `AGENTS.md`.

There is no account to fetch the history from, so **the log is the record**: `.data/film-log.csv`, written by hand
(`watched_on,title,year,rating,rewatch,note,tmdb_id,cinema,seat`; `seat` is where in the hall it was sat, as an NF Kino ticket says («Sal 1, rad 10, sete 7»; several tickets' seats as the mail lists them; empty where there is none): `film-sync seat <title> <date> <seat>` sets it, `scripts/ticket-seat.ts` reads it from the mail (NF Kino's text; Vega's eBillett PDF through `pdftotext`, where a ticket with no assigned seat gives «Sal, N billetter»; Cinemateket names none), and `mail-tickets.ts` writes it, also on a visit logged already, `#` starts a comment; an empty `watched_on` is a viewing whose day is not known: it counts as a film seen, but not in a day, month or year). `film-sync import-log` makes `film_watch` exactly
the log's rows; a line changed or deleted in the log is changed or deleted in the database. `film-sync add <title> <year>
<date> <rating>` (`-` for none) appends a line and imports.

What the owner **has or wants** is the collection: `.data/film-collection.csv`, written by hand (`added_on,title,year,status,rating,note,tmdb_id,format`; `status` is `owned` or `want`, empty means `owned`). `format` is what the owner has (`blu-ray`, `dvd`, `digital`; free text, lowercase) and is shown as «Eier» on the work's page. `film-sync import-collection` makes `film_collection` exactly its rows, and `tmdb` looks its titles up too. A film the log lacks that is owned or wanted has a page of its own, with the collection's lines (form, the day added, rating; never the note) and no people. Film has no library page (`/bibliotek` was removed, and answers with a redirect to the front page, as `/franchiser` does).

| `scripts/mail-ingest.ts`, `mail-tickets.ts`, `mail-service.ts` | A ticket mail from NF Kino (`@billett.nfkino.no`) or from Vega Scene through ebillett.io (`post@ebillett.io`, subject «kvittering og billetter»; `ebillett-ticket.ts` of the Nextcloud repository, UID `ebillett-<purchase>`), through Proton Mail Bridge on this machine, lands unchanged under `.data/raw/mail/`; its film, cinema and times go to the Kino calendar (`kino-ticket.ts` of the Nextcloud repository, one event per ticket, and none when the calendar has that film on that day already) and the log (`film-sync add`, once per film and day, with the cinema where `film-cinemas.csv` knows it), then `tmdb` matches it, `trakt` sends it (a visit logged ahead of its day goes with the daily run once the day has come) and film and forside are published at once (`publish-timers.ts`, which still checks that the sync finished well and that the snapshot changed), not at the next timer. `npm run mail:setup` writes `~/.config/mail-ingest.env`, `npm run mail:service -- install` keeps it running. A hall has one name wherever a ticket spells it otherwise (`canonicalHall` in `scripts/ticket-seat.ts`): Vega's three halls are «Sal 1» to «Sal 3» (its mails say «Vega kino tre» by turns), Odeon's IMAX is «IMAX» whoever sponsors it, Colosseum's «Samsung Galaxy» is «Sal 1». A ticket to leave out (returned, or a showing the log already has under another day) is a line in `.data/ticket-ignore.txt` (the event UID, `odeon-<reference>`). A ticket matches the log's line for its day by title without case, accents and punctuation, and writes the cinema on a line that has none The ticket is logged on its screening day, so a future day sits in the log until it has come, and `trakt` sends no viewing dated after today (`seen_until`) |
| `sync-rust/src/franchises.rs` | `film-sync franchises`: for each `collection_id` of `film_title`, TMDB's collection with every part and its release day (`film_franchise`, `film_franchise_part`; the name in Norwegian else English, landed under `.data/raw/tmdb/`), fetched again after a week. `film-sync related` (and `tv-sync related`) fills `film_related`/`tv_related`, the works TMDB recommends beside each, for the works stored without any (the daily `tmdb` appends them to every details answer). Film has no page of franchises (`/franchiser` was removed); a franchise is only a card on a film's own page: the franchise's own wide picture (`film_franchise.backdrop_path`) blurred behind the card and faded into the theme's background so the words keep their contrast, and the parts one under the other with their poster, the first at the top («Første del») and the newest at the bottom («Siste del») (`--everything` on `franchises` fetches them all again; `npm run snapshot` lands the pictures); a film's and a series' page (`packages/watch`, `extras.ts`, `pages/beside.tsx`) tells its franchise and its recommendations, each part or work «sett» when the log names it, undated viewings included |
| `sync-rust/src/tmdb.rs` | `film-sync tmdb`: looks each title and year up in TMDB (`TMDB_API_KEY`: a v3 key, or a v4 token starting «eyJ»), lands the answers under `.data/raw/tmdb/<date>/`, stores each film in Norwegian where TMDB has it (the title rule: `no-NO` is asked first, since TMDB keeps most Norwegian titles under `no` and a few under `nb`; `nb-NO` only when that gave the original's title; else the original's title, which is the English one for an English film and the original script for a Japanese one. `film-sync titles` fetches every stored film again after a change, without the people. Franchise names and recommendations follow the same order, and a franchise name TMDB has in no Norwegian is asked for in English) (`film_title` with ratings, money, the Norwegian age rating and release, the series and the ids on IMDb and Wikidata; `film_genre`, `film_country`, `film_company`, `film_keyword`, `film_credit` with the cast and the crew by job) and then every person of a credit (`film_person`: birth, place, biography in Norwegian or else English, IMDb id; the rest of what the register has per person is not kept). | `scripts/film-inbox.ts`, `film-inbox-plan.ts` | **Logg en film**: a Nextcloud **Forms** form named «Logg en film» (owner `ole`; why Forms and not Tables or our own island: `docs/CONVENTIONS.md`). Questions, matched by their text: **Dag** (date, required), **Tittel** (short text, required), **År** (short text, validation regex `^(\d{4})?$`), **Vurdering** (dropdown: `0,5`, `1`, `1,5` … `5`), **Kino** (short text), **Notat** (long text). Settings: no limit of one answer, anyone-with-the-link off, answers editable off. `npm run film:inbox [-- --dry-run]` reads the submissions with `podman exec nextcloud-database psql` (read only, no secret), checks each with `@designsystem/watch/film-log-entry` (the Rust importer's rules; a submission that breaks one is reported, not marked done, until it is deleted in Forms), appends the ones the log lacks (same day and title) to `.data/film-log.csv`, runs `import-log`, and remembers every valid submission id in `.data/film-forms-seen.txt` (so a line removed from the log does not come back). Commands from `apps/film`: `cargo run -p film-sync -- import-log | import-cinemas | add … | amend <title> <date> <year|-> <cinema|-> (sets year and cinema on the one line, the rest of the file untouched) | remove <title> <date|-> (takes the one line out, `-` for a line with no day) | trakt-login | trakt [--dry-run] | tmdb [--refresh | --everything]`, `npm run snapshot`, `npm run deploy`.
Timers: `schedule.json` runs `import-log` and `tmdb --refresh` daily: new films, and of the stored only what TMDB's change lists name since the last read (`film_tmdb_checked` keeps how far they vouch), plus at most 150 a run of those fetched before that; `--everything` fetches all (`node scripts/sync-timers.ts film install`, with `TMDB_API_KEY` in `~/.config/film-sync.env`, mode 600), and `node scripts/publish-timers.ts film install` publishes after it.
The pages show TMDB's credit (`TmdbNotice`): the terms ask for it, and for the page to say it is not endorsed by TMDB.
